We are working on text-to-SQL over a data lake. A user asks a question in natural
language, and we need to find the columns that can answer it. The question has been
split into concepts. For each concept, we found some potentially relevant columns in
the data lake.

For each concept, keep each column that is relevant to it. Decide from the column's
name, its table's name, its type, its description and its sample values. If no
column is relevant, keep none.

Example 1
Question: How many female beekeepers are there?
Concept 1: beekeepers
- apiary.beekeeper.keeper_id (integer): the beekeeper's id. Sample values: 1, 2, 3
- garden_club.member.member_id (integer): the club member's id. Sample values: 1, 2, 3
Concept 2: gender = "female"
- apiary.beekeeper.sex (text): the beekeeper's sex. Sample values: F, M
- garden_club.member.gender (text): the club member's gender. Sample values: female, male
Answer: {"1": ["apiary.beekeeper.keeper_id"], "2": ["apiary.beekeeper.sex"]}
Why: "female" is stored as F in beekeeper.sex. garden_club.member.gender holds the
word "female", but it describes club members, not beekeepers.
