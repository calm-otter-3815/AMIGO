We are working on text-to-SQL over a data lake. A user asks a question in natural
language. Split the question into the concepts it talks about: the things it asks for
and the things it filters on. Name each concept the way a database column would
describe it. Each concept should make sense on its own, without the question, so say
whose it is. The concepts of one question do not always belong to the same entity:
name each one after the entity it belongs to. You have not seen the data lake.

If the question gives a value for a concept, write the value on its own: a name or a
word as text, a number without units or comparison words, a date as a range of days.
A name standing alone is a value too: give it its own concept.

Example 1
Question: How many books written by Chinua Achebe were borrowed more than 5 times in 2023?
Answer: {"concepts": [
  {"concept": "book"},
  {"concept": "book's author", "text": "Chinua Achebe"},
  {"concept": "number of times a book was borrowed", "number": 5},
  {"concept": "book's borrowing date", "from": "2023-01-01", "to": "2023-12-31"}
]}
Why: "more than" is left out of the number. "in 2023" becomes every day of 2023.

Example 2
Question: Which airlines fly to cities with more than 1 million people?
Answer: {"concepts": [
  {"concept": "airline"},
  {"concept": "flight's destination city"},
  {"concept": "city's population", "number": 1000000}
]}
Why: the concepts belong to different entities. The destination belongs to the
flight, and the population belongs to the city, not to the airline.

Example 3
Question: List the customers with a premium membership who made a return since October 2021.
Answer: {"concepts": [
  {"concept": "customer"},
  {"concept": "customer's membership level", "text": "premium"},
  {"concept": "customer's returns"},
  {"concept": "customer's return date", "from": "2021-10-01"}
]}
Why: the value is "premium", not "premium membership": the word "membership" goes in
the concept. "since" gives a start date and no end date.

Example 4
Question: How fast can the Kestrel K7 go?
Answer: {"concepts": [
  {"concept": "vehicle's top speed"},
  {"concept": "vehicle's name", "text": "Kestrel K7"}
]}
Why: "Kestrel K7" is a name, so it gets its own concept, even though the question
never says it is a vehicle.

Example 5
Question: When was Dr. Mira Okafor hired?
Answer: {"concepts": [
  {"concept": "employee's hire date"},
  {"concept": "employee's name", "text": "Mira Okafor"}
]}
Why: a title like "Dr." is not part of the name.

Question: $question

Output Format, JSON only:
{"concepts": [{"concept": "<concept>", and at most one value: "text": "<text>" or
"number": <number> or "from": "YYYY-MM-DD" and/or "to": "YYYY-MM-DD"}]}

Return an answer only in this exact Output Format.
